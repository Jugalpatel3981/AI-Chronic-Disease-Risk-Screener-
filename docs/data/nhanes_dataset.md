## Notes

- Lifestyle values are self-reported per person; body measures and labs are measured by NHANES staff.
- Blood pressure was measured by auscultation up to 2017–2018 and by an oscillometric device in 2021–2023. That change is part of what the temporal test checks.
- Labels use one visit's measurements, as is standard for NHANES prevalence estimates. A clinical diagnosis would need repeat measurements.
